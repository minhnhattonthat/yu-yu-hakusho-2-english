# Style reference - Yu Yu Hakusho 2: Kakutou no Shou

Tone: the anime's English dub. Story pages are a brisk recap narrator (past tense, plain, a little punchy);
quotes are the characters' own voices. Readers know the series: use dub names and terms (glossary.md).

## Voices
| Who | Voice | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Yusuke | cocky street punk, short sentences, "Grandma" for Genkai | "Piece of cake!" / "Shut up! I'm just getting started!!" |
| Kuwabara | loud braggart, "the great Kuwabara", sore loser | "Now you know the power of the great Kuwabara!" |
| Kurama | calm, polite, faintly menacing | "It seems the match is mine..." |
| Hiei | curt, contemptuous, "Hn." | "Hn... Not even worth talking about." |
| Genkai | gruff old martial artist, dry sarcasm, "dimwit" | "Don't get cocky, you little twerp!!" |
| Toguro | quiet, cold, measured | "You lack something... a sense of danger." |

| Jin | cheerful country boy, folksy | "I win, y'all!" |
| Suzaku | arrogant demon lord | "Do you truly think you can defeat me!?" |
| Shishiwakamaru | vain, haughty swordsman | "Preposterous! You dare face me with that?" |
| Announcer (char select / tournament) | perky host | "And who will fight? Make your choice!" |

- M070 不良少年　浦飯幽助は、交通事故から子供をかばったことで、いのちをおとした。
  -> Yusuke Urameshi, juvenile delinquent, lost his life saving a child from a traffic accident.
- M071/M072 (one sentence split over two pages; keep the split, each page reads in sequence)
  よていがいの幽助の死にとまどう霊界であったがエンマ大王の息子コエンマは / 霊界たんていとしてはたらくということをじょうけんに、幽助を生き返らせた。
  -> Yusuke's unscheduled death threw Spirit World into confusion, but King Enma's son, Koenma, / brought Yusuke back to life, on the condition that he work as a Spirit Detective.
- M075 その指令は、霊界より妖怪に盗まれた三大秘宝『餓鬼玉』『暗黒鏡』『降魔の剣』をとりもどすこと。
  -> His mission: recover the Three Treasures that demons had stolen from Spirit World: the Orb of Baast, the Forlorn Hope and the Shadow Sword.
